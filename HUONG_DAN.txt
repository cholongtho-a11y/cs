GHI CHI SO DIEN NUOC - BAN 2 (cho Long Tho)
Trang ghi: https://cholongtho-a11y.github.io/cs/
May chu: Google Apps Script (Code.gs) gan vao Google Sheet.
Ban cu luu trong thu muc cu/.

TRIEN KHAI (lam 1 lan)
1. Mo Google Sheet > Tien ich mo rong > Apps Script > dan toan bo Code.gs moi > Luu.
2. Chay ham caiDat (cho phep quyen) de tao cac tab ChiSo, DauKy, NhanVien, CauHinh.
3. Tab NhanVien: nhap ten nhan vien cot TEN; menu "Chi so dien nuoc" > "Tao PIN" cap ma PIN.
4. Tab CauHinh: B1 = thang tieu thu (vi du 11/2026); B2 = MO hoac DONG; B3 = khoa cho Excel tu tai (chep vao CAI_DAT, khoa GC_AUTO_KHOA).
5. Trien khai > Quan ly trien khai > Sua > Phien ban moi > Trien khai (giu nguyen URL).
6. Excel: GHI_CS > "1b. Gui DS dau ky" (hoac dan file CSV vao tab DauKy), roi nhan vien quet tem, nhap PIN mot lan.
7. Sau khi ghi: GHI_CS > "3. Nap tu dong tu Google Sheet" > xu ly sheet KIEM_TRA_CS > Luu chi so.

QUET TEM LIEN TUC (ban 2.1)
- Tren trang ghi so bam nut xanh "QUET TEM BANG CAMERA", huong vao tem; trang tu mo dung sap. Khong can mo tab Camera moi.
- Lan dau Safari hoi quyen camera: chon Cho phep. Tich "Tu mo camera sau moi lan luu" de camera tu bat sau khi luu.
- Doc ma bang thu vien jsQR.js (cung thu muc, khong can mang ngoai). Sau khi cap nhat trang, xoa bo nho dem Safari neu van thay giao dien cu.
